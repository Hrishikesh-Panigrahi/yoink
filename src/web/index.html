Welcome to Yoink

Just yoink it from the swarm.

  • Search anywhere Stable APIs (Pirate Bay, YTS) are on by default. Region filter unlocks Bollywood, K-drama, anime, and more.
  • One-click downloads Hit Yoink it on any result. Track progress, pause, and open the folder from the Downloads tab.
  • Stays out of your way Minimizes to the system tray. Native notifications when a download finishes. Toggle either from Settings.
